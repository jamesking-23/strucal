02

An engineering workbench, not a form.

Parameters on the left, the member in the middle, the verification ledger below and the verdict on the right — the layout of a checking engineer's desk.

  1. AInspector

    Grouped parameters with inline units, instant validation and presets for concrete class, steel grade, rolled sections and ψ factors.

  2. BViewport

    A dimensioned cross-section and a loading diagram showing supports and actions — pan and zoom on a drafting grid.

  3. CLedger

    Every step with its clause, governing equation, substituted values and result, grouped by limit state.

  4. DVerdict

    Utilisation per check — emerald below 0.85, amber at or above 0.85, crimson when overstressed — and the governing limit state.
